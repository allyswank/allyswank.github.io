About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Dr. Sarah Davies' lab at Boston University. My research broadly focuses on understanding how genetic diversity and population structure can influence ecological and evolutionary trajectories of threatened species. Aside from my PhD research, I am passionate about strengthening the reciprocal relationship between science and society by integrating LGBTQ+ perspectives.
